## Gemini watermark alpha maps

`src/scan/detectors/gemini-alpha.js` contains alpha maps of Google Gemini's visible watermark, quantised to 8 bits. They are derived from captures published in:

- **GeminiWatermarkTool** by AllenK (Kwyshell). https://github.com/allenk/GeminiWatermarkTool
- **gemini-watermark-remover** by Jad (journey-ad / GargantuaX). https://github.com/GargantuaX/gemini-watermark-remover
